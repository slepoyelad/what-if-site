Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What If Question is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

  • It does not collect, store or share personal data of other people.
  • It stores the login tokens of the owner's own accounts as encrypted secrets, only to upload videos and read the owner's own post statistics.
  • Post statistics (views, likes and similar totals for our own videos) are kept privately to decide which topics to make next.
  • Nothing is sold or given to third parties.

To remove the tool's access, disconnect it in the Instagram or TikTok app settings. Contact: through the @what_if_question profile.
